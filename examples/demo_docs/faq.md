# FAQ

## How do I contact support?

Email support@example.com or open a ticket in the app. Response time is usually under one business day on weekdays.

## Do you offer student discounts?

Yes. Verified students get 20% off annual plans. Apply from the billing page with a school email.

## Where is the return policy written down?

Open the customer handbook. The Returns chapter is the source of truth; this FAQ does not restate those rules.
