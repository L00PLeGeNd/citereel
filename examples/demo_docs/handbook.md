# Customer Handbook

## Refunds

The refund window is **14 days** from purchase. Customers may request a full refund in that window if the product has not been activated.

Requests filed after the window closes are reviewed case by case. Approved late requests usually receive store credit rather than cash.

Activated licenses, gift cards, and promotional bundles are not refundable after the first successful login.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days inside the country. Expedited shipping is available at checkout for most regions.

Damaged shipments must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the packaging.

## Account

Keep the email on the order up to date. Support uses that address to verify ownership before changing billing or cancelling a plan.
